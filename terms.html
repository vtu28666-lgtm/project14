3. Approximate Arithmetic & Error Bound Guarantees

Mathematical precision models: 6-Bit, 10-Bit & 16-Bit modes

ApproxMAC.AI utilizes dynamic bit-level truncation within its Multiply-Accumulate (MAC) arithmetic pipelines to balance silicon energy efficiency against calculation fidelity:

  • Aggressive Approx (6-Bit Truncation): Active during baseline steady-state vibrations; yields up to 58.6% silicon energy reduction while maintaining SNR > 38 dB.
